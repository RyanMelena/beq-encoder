{% if best %}
How the best track is chosen

Tracks are ranked by audio quality: lossless with objects first, then lossless, then lossy with objects, then lossy. Ties go to the track with more channels, then the original language, then the default flag. Commentary, descriptive and forced tracks are skipped.

The new BEQ track is always TrueHD. It is checked sample by sample against the filtered source, and for TrueHD Atmos and DD+ Atmos every object's position is checked too. DD+ Atmos objects come from Cavern's decode of the lossy DD+ track. DTS:X is not supported, since it can't be converted without losing quality.
